We help departments use data science to solve pressing business problems. Using existing data and resources, we collaborate with departments to:

  • Define a pressing business problem
  • Develop a plan to use data science to help solve the problem
  • Hand off a solution that leads to more efficient and effective work

At the end of the project, your department will own and maintain the work on your own.

How data science can help your team

Many departments face common business challenges that data science can help solve. Data science can help your team:

  • Find the needle in the haystack
  • Prioritize and reduce your backlogs
  • Flag “stuff” early
  • A/B test something
  • Optimize resources

How to know your team is a good fit for this service

Departments interested in applying for this service should be prepared to:

  • Clearly explain the problem they are hoping to address
  • Get buy-in from across the department, including leadership, program staff, IT, and communications.
  • Identify staff who can help us find and understand data related to the problem
  • Participate actively throughout the engagement
  • Work within the established project timeline, with engagements typically lasting about 6 months.
  • Commit to public shareouts highlighting the impact and value of the work
